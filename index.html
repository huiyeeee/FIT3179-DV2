Chapter 1 · Overall performance

Two in three students meet expectations. The headline hides a lot.

NAPLAN isn't a pass or fail test. Since 2023, each student is placed in one of four proficiency levels, and "Strong" is the level that means meeting expectations for their year level. Let's see where Australia's students sit relative to that line, and where it gets more interesting.

One territory stands apart, and the reason is mostly geography.

Australia's states and territories are mostly within a few points of each other. One is not. Let's see who is ahead, who is behind, how consistent the gaps are, and what really lies behind them.

What lies behind the gaps?

Is it the state, or the setting?

Australia's students do very differently depending on whether they live in a city, a regional town or a remote community.
